As a form of initiation, I believe that writing this, through something simpler and without too much formality, is an attempt to communicate with **you**. Therefore, I ask permission for this and many other "little chats."

I believe that the way technology and connectivity have been spreading, with research pointing to evident risks, from childhood and the learning process to young people, adults, elderly women... all affected by the recent hyperconnectivity. In just a few decades, we went from being people with a certain "difficulty" in being tracked to being connected 24h / 7 days a week. This is the new habitual, the common. But it cannot be normalized. Our biochemical structure does not evolve quickly enough to keep up with large technology companies, their devices, and new forms of neural rewards, a social structure organized within these companies, with a financial flow that fuels people’s interest in dedicating themselves, increasingly, to gaining engagement... This distances us from the genuine exchanges that formed the foundations of social organization and of individual and collective identity construction. An accumulation of promises from modern times that arrived to make our lives easier, but with a cost that is often not read between the lines of "_I agree to the terms of the contract_". People transformed into data, data that circulates, but an internet that is increasingly empty and dying.

Within this entanglement of niches, with increasingly private layers controlled by entities whose moral choices are questionable, we need spaces in which we can exchange knowledge without worrying about an advertisement appearing at any moment, without worrying about the use of our data... The internet increasingly lacks places that remind us that we should not call such networks social, but rather: **_Digital Networks_**. On applications, or here, we are not socializing; we are digitizing our thoughts.
This website was built in this way, with ideas and movement. However, this exchange may even be seen as a form of socializing, but not in the sense commonly promoted regarding "social networks" or **_Digital Networks._** That is why I say that the publications made here are forms of expression that found no other place in which to express themselves freely and with the reach of the internet.
Here, without necessarily following writing rules, standards, formats... Regardless of engagement, without worrying and without seeking to go viral. Knowledge does need to be disseminated, but something that is at its beginning needs to go through its initial process. Abrupt ruptures tend to generate a dysregulation of our sense of the present moment. Understanding the now, understanding what has passed and what will come.. As mentioned regarding the evolution of connectivity, we see patterns that are fed by culture, environment, customs, standardization, algorithms, and many other variables that disrupt the human compass as it tries to locate itself within the comprehensible universe and its many different singularities.

I share these thoughts with you in an attempt to express my concern about how important it is to understand that one person makes a difference, but the collective produces transformation. Thinking about the spread of a search for grounding in ancestral knowledge, with truths necessary for biological, psychological, and cosmological understanding; on a planet with layers of connections that form an Ecosystem, a Biosphere... all of this reveals the need to _rethink_ "**THINKING**". The way our minds organize our ideas in a predominantly capitalist world, with market structures and governments managing this biological mass of more than 8 billion human beings... **_Shaping the structure of thought is the foundation of social human formation._** Whether through interactions between individuals, through the theological structures of groups, or through other ways of thinking and interacting... it has always been through Thinking that we have seen social formations, customs, rites, myths, prejudices, disputes, etc.... **_Thinking is the non-form that shapes the world of forms._**

**_I hope that this space can contribute to different ways of thinking and that those who read it will be able to reflect on these subjects, which are not always visited by our attention._**
